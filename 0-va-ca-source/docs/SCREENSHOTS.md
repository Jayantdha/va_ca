# Screenshots checklist

Take these on your phone from the demo link, then put the files in `docs/screens/` with exactly these names.

| File | Screen | How to get there |
|---|---|---|
| `01-login.png` | Log in / Sign up | Open the link |
| `02-discover.png` | Discover (businessman) | Sign up as Businessman |
| `03-profile.png` | Influencer profile | Tap the big photo |
| `04-chat.png` | Chat | Chat tab |
| `05-influencer.png` | Influencer home | Add `#demo` to the link, tap "My profile" under Influencer |
| `06-payment.png` | Payment, Pending then Received | Under Influencer tap "Payment", enter an amount, tap Pay |

Tips: use the phone's built-in screenshot, not a photo of the screen. Keep the battery and notification bar clean. Do not show real phone numbers.

When all six are in the folder, open `README.md` and remove the `<!--` and `-->` lines around the screenshot table.
Also put one or two of them into `project-page/index.html` (see the TODO comments there).
